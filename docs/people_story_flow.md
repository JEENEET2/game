# People, Story, and Game Flow

## Main cast

### Ayaan Dar

The player character. Ayaan returns home after years away. He knows the valley's language, paths, and customs, but he is no longer fully trusted because he left. His journey is both practical and personal.

### Zooni Dar

Ayaan's younger cousin. She manages the family house and is the first person who tells him what changed. She becomes the home-base manager and helps track repairs, stored supplies, and village requests.

### Ghulam Nabi

An old carpenter from the lakeside district. He teaches repair, timber selection, and old house construction. His quests introduce dhajji dewari-inspired structures, roof repairs, and tool crafting.

### Rafiq

A shepherd in the Pahalgam-inspired river valley. He teaches animal handling, grazing routes, and how to read weather by animal behavior.

### Nargis

A trader who moves between Srinagar, Budgam, Baramulla, and Jammu-inspired areas. She introduces the economy, barter, long road risks, and supply missions.

### Professor Kaul

A retired teacher and amateur historian. He helps decode Ayaan's father's notebook and marks old routes, shrines, and abandoned houses on the map.

### Bashir Dar

Ayaan's missing father. He was studying unusual winter patterns, blocked routes, and old shelters in the high passes. His notes become the thread that pulls the player deeper into the mountains.

## Factions and social groups

- Lakeside traders.
- Orchard farmers.
- Shepherd families.
- Mountain guides.
- Carpenters and masons.
- Road workers.
- Winter rescue volunteers.

The player earns trust with people by solving practical problems, not by filling abstract bars. A family remembers if the player saved their sheep, repaired their roof, or delivered medicine before snow closed the road.

## Story acts

### Act 1: Return

Ayaan comes home after the first big autumn storm. The player learns basic movement, repairs a small bridge, recovers the family notebook, and restores the first room of the old house.

End of act: Ayaan finds a map page showing his father's last planned route.

### Act 2: The Valley Opens

The player travels between villages, solves local needs, gathers tools, and learns seasonal systems. Each region gives one key ability or piece of knowledge.

- Lake District: trading and navigation.
- Orchard Plain: food, storage, and village work.
- Shepherd River: animals, rivers, and forest routes.
- Flower Meadow: snow, climbing, and cold.
- Trade Road: supply movement.

End of act: The player learns that Bashir reached the high passes before disappearing.

### Act 3: Before Winter

The valley enters late autumn. The player chooses where to send limited supplies, which routes to repair first, and which families to help before snow arrives.

End of act: The main pass closes early, forcing a dangerous alternate route.

### Act 4: White Road

The player crosses the winter high country. Visibility, cold, slope physics, and navigation matter. The story becomes quiet and tense.

End of act: The player finds Bashir's final shelter and understands what he was trying to protect.

### Act 5: Spring Melt

The player returns as snow melts. Earlier choices affect which routes reopen, which villages are stable, and what kind of future the valley has.

## Mission structure

Missions should be built from real actions:

- Carry beams to a damaged roof.
- Move stones away from a channel.
- Guide sheep away from unstable snow.
- Find a missing cow during fog.
- Repair a bridge with the right materials.
- Bring medicine before a road closes.
- Mark a safe route through deep snow.

Avoid missions where the player only follows glowing arrows. The world itself should communicate.

## Example first quest

Title: The Broken Crossing

Flow:

1. Ayaan reaches a small canal bridge blocked by storm debris.
2. Ghulam Nabi asks him to clear fallen branches.
3. The player pushes, lifts, and drags light physics objects.
4. The player collects two timber planks.
5. The bridge is repaired enough to cross.
6. A new path to the family house opens.

Purpose:

- Teach movement.
- Teach interaction.
- Show physical world rules.
- Introduce old construction and repair theme.

## Example animal quest

Title: Rafiq's Stray Sheep

Flow:

1. The player reaches the river valley.
2. Rafiq says three sheep scattered after thunder.
3. The player approaches calmly and guides them back.
4. Fast movement scares sheep; crouching and slow walking works better.
5. One sheep is near a dangerous riverbank.

Purpose:

- Teach animal AI.
- Teach sound/visibility behavior.
- Teach river danger.
