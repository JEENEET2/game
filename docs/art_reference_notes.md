# Art Reference Notes

## Visual goal

The world should look like a Kashmir-inspired living valley: not fantasy, not generic alpine, and not tourist-postcard only. The art direction should show ordinary work, old and new houses, mud paths, fields, rivers, power lines, shops, animals, and weathered materials.

## Terrain

- Snow mountains in the distance.
- Green summer meadows.
- Autumn chinar leaves in red, gold, and orange.
- River valleys with boulders and pine/deodar forests.
- Orchard plains.
- Warmer foothill palette for the Jammu-inspired region.

## Trees

Chinar:

- Large trunk and broad canopy.
- Strong seasonal identity.
- Autumn leaf carpets are important.

Pine/deodar:

- Used in higher valleys and river forests.
- Darker silhouettes against snow.

Orchard trees:

- Apple orchards in Budgam-inspired villages.
- Seasonal fruit and blossoms.

## Houses

Old Kashmiri-inspired houses:

- Timber frame with masonry or mud/brick infill.
- Dhajji dewari-inspired patchwork wall rhythm.
- Taq-inspired masonry and timber bands where appropriate.
- Sloped roofs for snow and rain.
- Wooden windows, lattice details, balconies, and weathered gates.

Modern houses:

- Concrete and brick.
- Painted exterior walls.
- Tin or flat roof variations.
- Balconies, metal railings, water tanks, roadside shops.

Village props:

- Wood piles.
- Hay bundles.
- Woven baskets.
- Small fences.
- Hand carts.
- Animal troughs.
- Prayer flags are not a Kashmir default and should not be used as generic mountain decoration.

## Animals

Prototype animals:

- Sheep.
- Cows.

Later animals:

- Ponies/horses.
- Dogs.
- Birds.
- Wildlife in forest and high mountain areas.

## Clothing

The prototype can use simple outdoor clothing. Later art should include regional cold-weather garments, work clothing, rain layers, and modern mixed dress. Avoid costume-like exaggeration.
