# Asset Manifest

This document lists the first assets needed for the prototype and marks whether they are placeholder, custom, scanned, recorded, or licensed.

## Characters

### Ayaan prototype character

Type: 3D character.

Needed:

- Human body mesh.
- Outdoor clothing.
- Basic face and hair.
- Third-person animation rig.

Prototype approach:

- Use a clean placeholder humanoid until final character art is ready.

### Villager NPC set

Type: modular characters.

Needed:

- Elder carpenter.
- Cousin/home-base character.
- Shepherd.
- Trader.
- Teacher/historian.

Prototype approach:

- Use simple silhouettes with different clothing colors and props.

## Animals

### Sheep

Needed:

- Mesh.
- Idle graze animation.
- Walk/trot animation.
- Startled animation.
- Bleat audio.

Gameplay:

- Herding and rescue quests.

### Cow

Needed:

- Mesh.
- Idle graze animation.
- Slow walk animation.
- Lowing audio.

Gameplay:

- Village life, farming tasks, animal shelter quests.

## Buildings

### Old Kashmiri-inspired house

Features:

- Timber frame rhythm.
- Brick/mud infill.
- Sloped roof.
- Wooden window details.
- Weathered gate.

Gameplay:

- Family house restoration.
- Village interiors later.

### Modern valley house

Features:

- Concrete or brick walls.
- Painted exterior.
- Tin roof or flat roof variant.
- Balcony and railings.

Gameplay:

- Villages should feel current, not frozen in the past.

### Roadside shop

Features:

- Shutter.
- Signboard.
- Small counter.
- Stacked crates.

Gameplay:

- Trading and supply missions.

## Nature

### Chinar tree

Variants:

- Spring/summer green.
- Autumn red/gold.
- Winter bare.

Needs:

- Large tree mesh.
- Leaf material variants.
- Fallen leaf ground decal or mesh scatter.

### Pine/deodar tree

Needs:

- Multiple heights.
- Snow-covered variant.
- Wind animation.

### Orchard trees

Needs:

- Blossom variant.
- Fruit variant.
- Bare winter variant.
