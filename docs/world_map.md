# World Map Plan

## Map philosophy

The map should be inspired by real Kashmir and nearby terrain patterns, but not copied one-to-one. We use recognizable moods and geography while keeping the world fictional for gameplay freedom.

The playable world is one connected valley network:

- Lakeside city basin.
- Orchard villages and fields.
- River valleys.
- Snow meadows.
- Mountain roads.
- High passes.
- Warmer southern foothills.

## Major regions

### Srinagar-inspired region: Lake District

Role: opening city, trading hub, family history.

Key features:

- Lake edges, canals, wooden bridges, markets.
- Old city lanes and mixed old/new houses.
- Houseboats and gardens later in production.
- Chinar trees along water and roads.

Gameplay:

- Tutorial repairs.
- First vendors.
- Story archive and family contacts.
- Boat travel later.

### Gulmarg-inspired region: Flower Meadow

Role: alpine meadow, snow sports, winter survival.

Reference notes: Gulmarg is known as a meadow destination and winter sports area, with snow from winter months and high mountain access.

Key features:

- Wide meadow surrounded by pine forest.
- Snow slopes and high ridges.
- Cable route equivalent later, if needed.
- Pony routes and tourist cabins.

Gameplay:

- Snow movement.
- Avalanche-risk areas.
- Ski/sled prototype later.
- Cold exposure and visibility systems.

### Pahalgam-inspired region: Shepherd River

Role: river valley, shepherds, animal systems.

Reference notes: Pahalgam is strongly tied to the Lidder River valley and shepherd identity. The Lidder River is a glacial river flowing through the Pahalgam area.

Key features:

- Fast river with boulders.
- Pine and deodar forest.
- Meadows, shepherd trails, small bridges.
- Fishing spots.

Gameplay:

- Herding sheep.
- River crossing.
- Trout fishing later.
- Rescue and supply quests.

### Budgam-inspired region: Orchard Plain

Role: farming, village life, food economy.

Key features:

- Apple orchards.
- Low hills.
- Mud paths, fields, water channels.
- Traditional and modern homes mixed together.

Gameplay:

- Harvesting.
- Animal shelter repairs.
- Village reputation.
- Crafting ingredients.

### Baramulla-inspired region: Trade Road

Role: road hub, bridges, mountain gate.

Key features:

- River crossing town.
- Road checkpoints.
- Warehouses and old bridges.
- Slopes toward high country.

Gameplay:

- Supply movement.
- Cart/pack animal missions.
- Access to northern routes.

### Jammu-inspired region: Warm Foothills

Role: climate contrast and late-game supply route.

Key features:

- Warmer colors.
- Lower hills and wider plains.
- Stone roads and market lanes.
- Different tree and building palette.

Gameplay:

- Heat and water management later.
- Long-distance travel.
- Story contrast between valley and foothills.

### High Passes

Role: danger, mystery, final story path.

Key features:

- Glaciers, cliffs, scree, caves.
- Thin vegetation.
- Harsh wind and snow.
- Ruined shelters.

Gameplay:

- Advanced climbing.
- Severe cold.
- Whiteout navigation.
- Father's trail.

## Travel design

The world should not be empty. Every route needs one or more reasons to travel:

- A practical task.
- A scenic reward.
- A resource.
- A shortcut.
- A story clue.
- A weather risk.

## Scale target

Prototype scale:

- 1 compact valley with 3 settlements.
- 1 river.
- 1 meadow.
- 1 hill forest.
- 1 mountain pass entrance.

Full game scale:

- 6 major regions.
- 20 to 30 named settlements and landmarks.
- Streaming terrain chunks.
- Separate seasonal state per biome.
