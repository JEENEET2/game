# Valleybound Game Design

## High concept

Valleybound is an open-world survival adventure about returning to a mountain valley after a long absence and helping its people prepare for a hard winter. The world is fictional, but the terrain, houses, trees, animals, seasons, and travel routes are inspired by Kashmir: Srinagar-like waterways, Gulmarg-like alpine meadows, Pahalgam-like river valleys, Budgam-like orchards and villages, Baramulla-like mountain roads, Jammu-like warmer foothills, and high passes above the tree line.

The game should feel human, physical, and lived in. Movement has weight. Snow slows the player. Rivers push against the body. Stones roll downhill. Animals react to sound and weather. Villagers have daily work instead of standing still forever.

## Player fantasy

The player is not a superhero. They are capable, local, and practical: someone who knows mountain paths, can repair tools, calm animals, cut wood, talk to elders, cross rivers, climb ridges, and make decisions that affect the valley.

The pleasure of the game comes from:

- Travelling across a beautiful but demanding landscape.
- Understanding the needs of different villages.
- Solving grounded problems with tools, physics, weather, and local knowledge.
- Watching the valley change through spring, summer, autumn, and winter.

## Main character

Working name: Ayaan Dar.

Ayaan returns to the valley after several years away. His family home is damaged, his father is missing from an old mountain route, and the villages are under pressure from storms, blocked roads, failing bridges, and animals moving strangely from the high forest.

The character should be customizable later, but the prototype uses one human body rig with realistic movement and outdoor clothing.

Core traits:

- Practical, quiet, observant.
- Knows local terrain but has been away long enough to rediscover it with the player.
- Carries a small field notebook used for map marks, recipes, sketches, and quest notes.

## Story premise

An early autumn storm damages bridges, footpaths, orchards, and animal shelters across the valley. At the same time, old family notes suggest that Ayaan's father disappeared while investigating unusual winter patterns near the high passes.

The main story is about rebuilding trust between settlements, preparing the valley for winter, and following the father's trail into the mountains.

The story should avoid melodrama. It should feel like a grounded mountain life adventure: family, land, work, weather, memory, and responsibility.

## Game flow

### Opening hour

1. Ayaan arrives near the Srinagar-inspired lakeside district.
2. The player learns walking, camera control, interaction, and inventory.
3. A bridge on a small canal is broken by storm debris.
4. The player gathers wood, moves physics objects, and helps repair the crossing.
5. A village elder sends Ayaan toward the river valley to check on shepherds.

### Early game

- Explore the first valley region.
- Help repair paths and shelters.
- Learn animal handling with sheep and cows.
- Unlock the field notebook and local map.
- Reach the first old family house and restore it as a base.

### Mid game

- Travel between Srinagar, Budgam, Baramulla, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam-inspired zones.
- Unlock seasonal systems and mountain gear.
- Build trust with villages by solving practical problems.
- Track rumors about the missing father.

### Late game

- Survive deep winter travel.
- Cross high passes with limited visibility and dangerous terrain.
- Discover why the old routes were closed.
- Make choices about which villages receive supplies before the worst storm.

## Core loop

Explore -> observe problem -> gather or craft -> use physics/tool/weather knowledge -> help people or open route -> earn trust/resources -> travel farther.

## Activities

- Walking, running, jumping, crouching, climbing, swimming.
- Gathering wood, herbs, fruit, stones, wool, milk, and metal scraps.
- Repairing bridges, fences, roofs, and animal shelters.
- Herding sheep and cows.
- Fishing and cooking.
- Talking with villagers.
- Trading in small markets.
- Reading signs of weather, animal tracks, and river flow.
- Discovering shrines, old houses, caves, and mountain viewpoints.

## Tone

Quiet, grounded, beautiful, sometimes dangerous. The valley should invite the player in, then make them respect it.
